# Privacy Policy — Block Forge: Kingdom

Effective Date: 2026-11-18

**Block Forge: Kingdom** ("the Game") respects your privacy.

## 1. Data Collection & Storage
- All game progress (high scores, adventure levels, kingdom upgrades, and settings) is stored **locally on your device** using standard storage (`SharedPreferences`).
- The Game does **not** require account registration and does **not** collect, transmit, or sell personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location).

## 2. Third-Party Services (Optional Future Integrations)
- In its base build, the Game contains **no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs**.
- If you later integrate Google AdMob or Google Play Games Services before publishing, you must update this Privacy Policy and your Google Play Console Data Safety declaration accordingly.

## 3. Children's Privacy
- The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
